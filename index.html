Questions

Do I pay on this website?

No. There is no payment page. After you send your order on WhatsApp, our team confirms it and shares payment options in the chat. Nothing is charged before you confirm.

What if WhatsApp does not open?

After you place an order, use the Open WhatsApp button on the confirmation screen. You can also copy the order message there and paste it into a chat with us.

Can I change the parts?

You can add memory, storage and extras on each rig page. For any other change, such as a different graphics card or case, message us on WhatsApp and we will quote it.

How accurate are the frame rates?

They are estimates at high settings with ray tracing off. Real results depend on the game version, drivers and your settings.

Can I cancel after sending the order?

Yes, any time before you confirm payment with our team in the chat.

What warranty do I get?

Every rig includes a 1-year system warranty. You can extend it to 3 years as an add-on on the rig page.
